Analog Waveguide - Firmware Updater

WebUSB DFU flashing tool. Runs entirely in the browser.

Unsupported browser

This tool needs WebUSB, available in Chrome, Edge, and other Chromium-based browsers (not Firefox or Safari). It also needs a secure context — this page must be served over https:// or http://localhost (GitHub Pages serves HTTPS by default).
No live firmware-version check is performed — please confirm you're flashing the right release before starting. To check the currently installed version, press the factory settings button on the right side of the bottom board, next to the USB-C port. The menu entry “Show firmware version” shows the currently installed firmware version along with the build date.
